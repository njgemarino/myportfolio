About Me

I am an IT student with a strong passion for web development and a growing interest in creating visually appealing, functional, and user-centered digital experiences. My journey began with learning the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript, which I continue to improve through hands-on projects and continuous practice. As a working student, I have developed discipline, independence, and strong time management skills, allowing me to balance academics, personal responsibilities, and skill development effectively. My experience in front-end development and quality assurance has helped me become detail-oriented, ensuring that websites are usable, consistent, and accessible across different devices. My goal is to grow as a web developer and create meaningful digital experiences that are both visually engaging and technically sound, always keeping the user at the center of the design process while continuously learning and embracing new challenges.
